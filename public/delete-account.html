How to Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your HHF Social account directly from the app. Follow the steps below:

  1. Open the HHF Social app and log in to your account.
  2. Go to your Profile tab (bottom right).
  3. Tap the ⋮ menu icon in the top right corner.
  4. Select "Delete Account" from the menu.
  5. Enter your password to confirm your identity.
  6. Tap "Delete Forever" to permanently delete your account.

⚠️ What Gets Deleted

  • Your profile and personal information
  • All your posts, photos, and comments
  • Your direct messages and chat history
  • Your followers and following lists
  • Your notification history

This action is permanent and irreversible. Once your account is deleted, it cannot be recovered. Please make sure you want to proceed before confirming.
