Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) — Last updated: April 9, 2026

⚠️ Zero Tolerance Policy

HHF Social maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy toward any form of child sexual abuse material (CSAM), child sexual exploitation, or any content that sexualizes, endangers, or harms minors.

1 Our Commitment

HHF Social is committed to maintaining a safe platform free from child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We take the safety of minors extremely seriously and have established standards, tools, and processes to prevent, detect, and respond to any such content or behavior on our platform.

2 Prohibited Content & Behavior

The following is strictly prohibited on HHF Social:

  • Any imagery, video, or text that depicts, promotes, or glorifies the sexual abuse or exploitation of children.
  • Sharing, distributing, or soliciting child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form.
  • Any content that sexualizes minors, including fictional depictions.
  • Grooming behavior — building a relationship with a minor for the purpose of sexual exploitation or abuse.
  • Solicitation of sexual content from or involving minors.
  • Sextortion or coercion targeting minors.
  • Any discussion or coordination of sexual acts involving minors.
  • Sharing personal or identifying information of minors without parental consent.

3 Age Requirement

Users must be at least 13 years of age to create an account on HHF Social. We do not knowingly allow users under this age to register. Accounts found to belong to users under 13 will be immediately terminated and all associated data will be deleted.

4 Detection & Prevention

We employ the following measures to prevent and detect CSAE content:

  • User Reporting: All users can report content and accounts that violate these standards through our in-app reporting system (long-press any message or visit a user's profile to report).
  • Content Review: Reports are reviewed promptly, and appropriate action is taken, including immediate content removal and account termination.
  • Account Termination: Any account found to be sharing CSAE content will be permanently banned without warning.
  • Blocking Tools: Users can block other users to immediately prevent further contact.

5 Enforcement & Consequences

When CSAE content or behavior is identified, we take the following actions:

  • Immediate removal of the offending content.
  • Permanent termination of the offender's account.
  • Reporting to authorities: We will report all identified instances of CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline, and/or to relevant local law enforcement agencies as required by law.
  • Preservation of evidence: Relevant account data and content may be preserved and shared with law enforcement to aid in investigations.

6 How to Report

If you encounter any content or behavior on HHF Social that involves or suggests the sexual abuse or exploitation of a child, please report it immediately:

  • In-App: Long-press any message and select "Report," or visit the user's profile and tap the report button.
  • Email: Contact us directly at ken@hhfmedia.com with the subject line "CSAE Report."
  • NCMEC CyberTipline: You can also report directly to NCMEC.

All reports are treated with the highest priority and will be investigated promptly.

7 Cooperation with Law Enforcement

HHF Social cooperates fully with law enforcement agencies investigating CSAE. We comply with all applicable laws and regulations regarding the reporting and handling of child exploitation material, including the requirements of the PROTECT Our Children Act and other applicable legislation.

8 Updates to These Standards

We regularly review and update these standards to ensure they reflect best practices and comply with evolving legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision.
